Privacy Policy

KnowYourResume ("we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to protecting your career data and personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, safeguard, and respect your information when you use our AI Career Operating System.

Our Core Privacy Guarantee: We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information or resume drafts. Furthermore, we never use your private resume content to train public or commercial foundation AI models.

1. Information We Process

  • Profile & Career Information: Full name, contact details (email, phone, location), professional summary, employment history, education, skills, projects, and certifications entered into the Resume Builder.
  • Uploaded Documents: Resumes uploaded in PDF, DOCX, or TXT formats for ATS scanning. All text extraction is executed on-device in your browser; your original raw files are never stored on our server storage disks.
  • Job Descriptions: Target job vacancy postings you provide to compute deterministic ATS keyword scores and skill gap analyses.
  • Authentication Identifiers: Email address, encrypted authentication tokens, and display names provided via Google OAuth or standard email registration.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To format and generate high-fidelity, ATS-compliant PDF and print resumes.
  • To calculate deterministic ATS keyword match ratios, category breakdowns, and skill gap metrics.
  • To generate tailored bullet points, executive summaries, and interview preparation questions through the Google Gemini API.
  • To synchronize and back up your saved drafts securely across your authenticated devices via Google Cloud Firestore.

3. On-Device Privacy Architecture

Whenever you upload a resume file for ATS analysis or builder import, text parsing is performed entirely inside your browser's local sandbox using compiled WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript (PDF.js / JSZip). Your binary document never leaves your machine unless you choose to sync your structured JSON draft to your private cloud workspace.

4. Artificial Intelligence Ethics & Processing

When you invoke AI assistance (e.g., bullet enhancement or summary generation), only the specific section text and optional job description are transmitted ephemerally to Google Gemini API over encrypted TLS 1.3 channels. Google Cloud does not retain or use your personal data to train generative models.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right of Access: You can view all saved resume versions and application records at any time.
  • Right to Portability: You can export your master resume data in standardized JSON format or industry-standard PDF at any moment without lock-in.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can clear all local storage drafts in one click or permanently delete your cloud account and stored drafts from Account Settings.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights.

6. Data Retention & Contact

Guest users retain data solely within their browser's local storage. Authenticated users' encrypted drafts remain stored in Google Cloud Firestore until manually deleted. For privacy inquiries or Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR), contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@knowyourresume.web.app.

Security Architecture & Data Protection

KnowYourResume is built with defense-in-depth principles to ensure that candidate resumes and personally identifiable information remain strictly private and secure.

1. Cryptographic Safeguards

  • TLS 1.3 Transport Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy.
  • AES-256 Storage Encryption: Cloud backups stored in Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 keys.

Our Cloud Firestore database rules strictly enforce ownership boundaries: only authenticated requests where request.auth.uid == resource.data.userId are permitted to read or write resume documents. Unauthorized cross-account access is cryptographically blocked at the database kernel.

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

Our ATS algorithm is 100% deterministic and follows the same parsing rules used by enterprise systems like Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever. It evaluates keyword frequency, exact/partial matches, skill categorization, and section completeness to generate an un-inflated compatibility percentage (0–100%).

Legacy ATS algorithms look for specific token matches. While our system resolves common industry synonyms (such as AWS ↔ Amazon Web Services or Postgres ↔ PostgreSQL), recruiters often configure strict keyword filters. We recommend explicitly including the exact terminology from the target Job Description in your skills and experience bullets.

A score of 75% or higher is considered competitive (Strong / Exceptional). Scores below 50% indicate substantial terminology misalignment with the job description. Targeting 80%+ maximizes your likelihood of passing initial recruiter screening filters.
